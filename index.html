GpxLens

Before a track goes to Strava, Komoot or your coach, see what the GPS actually recorded: true distance, real climbing, moving time, and the shape of the route.

Open GpxLens

Honest numbers

Haversine distance point-to-point, elevation gain and loss, moving time from the timestamps, average speed from the two.

See the route

A drawn map of every segment and an elevation profile - spot the GPS drift and the false summit before anyone else does.

Segment-aware

Multi-segment and multi-track files break down per segment, with gaps between them not counted as distance.

Nothing uploads

The parser runs in your browser against local text. Your tracks never leave your machine.

What it reads

  • Tracks and segments: points, distance, climb, descent, min/max elevation, duration, average speed
  • Waypoints and routes, counted
  • Partial data called out: points missing elevation or timestamps produce warnings, not silent zeros
  • Malformed input: bad coordinates, unparseable timestamps, empty tracks, non-GPX files

Cross-checked against an independent oracle

The shared engine (engine.js) runs in Node against a corpus whose expected facts are computed by a separate Python implementation (ElementTree parse, its own haversine) in tests/build_corpus.py - two independent implementations of the same math, 70 checks (node tests/run_tests.js).
